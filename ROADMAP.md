# Sightline Prompter roadmap

What's planned and in which order. Joanne approved every item on 27 October
2026, apart from the one on hold. The [change log in the README](readme.md#change-log)
records what has shipped.

**How it's ordered:** first by **value**, meaning what makes recording to camera better, then by **effort**. Quick wins come first; long,
complex builds come later.

- **Value:** ★★★ changes recording day · ★★ clearly better · ★ nice to have
- **Size:** **S** about a day · **M** a few days · **L** a week or more

## Where it stands (28 October 2026)

**v1.0.3 is the latest release** (28 October 2026). It added the edit pack
(26), the clickable progress bar (37), check for updates (38), skills for
Claude Code and Codex, and see-through backing on air.

**Next:** 47 the components list, 20
teach it your words, 40 choose your own buttons, the sound-check meter (41)
and loudness per take (42). The full list,
in order, is under [To do](#to-do); everything built is under [Done](#done).
