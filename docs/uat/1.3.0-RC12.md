# RC12 combined UAT

12 November 2026. Use the RC12 launcher and its copied RC11 profile. Your
appearance, connections, controller assignments and occupied slots
should remain. Earlier recordings and script snapshots are unchanged.

## Five-minute check

1. On the small prompter screen, confirm Help, Settings and all four window
   controls stay together at the upper right. Check the slot picker. On a large
   screen, confirm the same alignment with nine slot buttons and full sliders.
2. In a disposable editable script, open the full editor with Ctrl+E. Its exit
   says **Close**. Make a change: it says **Cancel**. Undo to the saved text:
   **Close** returns. Repeat and Save. Cancel a Save As dialog and check that
   your draft remains. Samples still use **Back to prompter / Save a copy**.
3. Open Settings from the prompter's monitor. Move Settings to another monitor,
   close and reopen it; it should remember. Repeat with Prepare to record and
   the user guide. Test again after restarting Sightline. Native file dialogs
   follow their owner and the operating system's placement rules.
4. With command listening enabled, say an enabled button's displayed words in
   the active Sightline dialog, such as **Check again**, **Continue**, **Close**
   or the displayed next-step label. Look for the gold acknowledgement.
   Hidden, disabled, background and ambiguous buttons must not run. Native OS
   prompts still need their normal controls. Explicit recording permissions
   and confirmations still apply.
5. In Prepare to record, run the microphone check. Stay quiet for the room
   baseline, then wait before speaking: the voice countdown must wait too.
   Speak a short sentence with a breath. It should finish after you stop or at
   ten seconds of sampling. No speech is incomplete, never a passed check.
   Confirm the meter and results in Settings and on the prompter. Try the
   sample's separate room/voice checks too.
6. Under Voice, choose **Sightline** as the wake word and Apply. Say
   **Sightline, go**, **Sightline, pause** and **Sightline, retake**. Then restore
   your preferred wake word. Reading “Sightline Prompter” is not a command.
7. In Voice mode with **Start from section heading** enabled, test Start,
   Next, Section two, a soft `{stop}`, Retake and a paused quick edit. Read the
   heading as a lead-in, then the script. Retake owns the previous section
   until you actually start the next passage. With the option off, use the
   body-line position. Stops and timed pauses still hold Scroll mode.
8. Check your actual Windows microphone level before/after listening and the
   checks. Sightline requests AGC off and logs the actual input name and applied
   processing flags in the local support log; it never sets hardware gain.
   Other applications and drivers can still change it. Very brief marked or
   discarded takes retain raw readings but say **Too short to assess**;
   clipping is still reported.

## Boundaries of this candidate

Recorded-chunk replay recovers seven observed Retake misses and improves three
following windows by about three seconds. This is not proof of universal
recognition accuracy. Test your microphone and natural delivery. Live word
times remain navigation estimates; use a fresh transcript for cuts/captions.
The full command-training workflow remains on the roadmap.
