# Handling project personal information

**Internal procedure proposal — 29 October 2026. Not yet adopted.**

This is an operational companion to the privacy draft. It is not evidence
that the listed controls have already been implemented. An organisational
owner must approve and operate it before publication of related promises.

## Scope and responsibility

Confirm who controls project enquiries and who receives Stripe and Ko-fi
payments. Record the legal entity, contact, actual service providers and
people with access. Do not assume that a brand name identifies a controller.
Check whether the organisation's existing ICO registration, privacy notice
and record of processing already cover this work, and whether an update
or data-protection fee assessment is needed.

Distinguish the company's support and payment records from user-managed
scripts and recordings. The project does not operate a central copy of
those local files. If the company starts hosting scripts, receiving bulk
logs or providing managed transcription, assess that new activity before
offering it; do not stretch this notice to cover it silently.

## Minimise what comes in

Ask for the version, platform and a minimal reproduction first. Prefer
invented scripts and redacted screenshots. Never ask for a Deepgram key,
remote token, complete application-state file or payment-card credentials.
If a recording is necessary, agree a private route and purpose, and ask
the sender to remove unrelated people and confidential passages.

Do not copy support attachments into a public repository or send them
to an AI provider without an appropriate basis, notice and provider
assessment. Avoid requesting special-category information. If it arrives
unnecessarily, restrict access and assess whether it can be deleted;
do not assume an ordinary legitimate-interest basis satisfies Article 9.

## Document decisions and retention

Keep a small record of processing purposes, information categories,
recipients, lawful bases, security measures and retention. Complete an
ordinary legitimate-interests assessment for support and voluntary-payment
administration before relying on that basis. Marketing or a supporter wall
needs its own assessment and, where appropriate, separate consent.

Proposed schedule, subject to adoption:

| Record | Proposed handling |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary support correspondence | Delete or anonymise within 24 months after resolution |
| Troubleshooting files | Delete within 90 days after resolution unless an active investigation needs them |
| Company tax and accounting records | Keep six years from the relevant financial year end, or longer where required |
| Unneeded credentials received by mistake | Restrict access, arrange revocation with the sender and remove unnecessary copies promptly |
| Records relevant to an active dispute | Retain the necessary subset until the hold ends; document the reason |

Choose an owner and a review routine that can actually enforce these
periods. Inventory backups and service-provider copies, record their expiry
or deletion limits, and avoid promising immediate deletion from every copy.
Public GitHub history and user exports are separate from the company's
private retention schedule.

## Access and service providers

Use individual accounts, multifactor authentication and least access for
email, repositories and payment administration. Review access when a
helper leaves. Use appropriate device protection and backups. Record which
providers act as processors and which act as independent controllers;
check the relevant agreements rather than applying one label to them all.

Map actual hosting and international transfers. Where needed, document
adequacy or the applicable UK transfer mechanism and assessment. A provider's
privacy-policy link alone does not complete that assessment. Review changes
to payment integrations, Deepgram, AI support tools and the Marketplace.

## Rights requests and complaints

Log the request date, scope, identity checks and response deadline.
Acknowledge privately and ask only for information needed to identify
the records and requester. Normally respond within one month; use any
lawful extension or clarification process transparently. Search the
systems actually used, assess exemptions and retained legal records,
and explain the outcome and complaint route.

Do not claim to erase files on a user's computer or data held independently
by Stripe, Ko-fi or GitHub. Help the person identify the appropriate
organisation. Restrict internal circulation of identity documents and
delete verification material when no longer needed.

## Security incidents

Contain the incident, preserve proportionate evidence, revoke exposed
credentials and record what happened, whose information was affected,
likely consequences and remedial steps. Assess notification duties promptly.
Where UK GDPR requires notification to the ICO, the normal deadline is
72 hours after becoming aware; document reasons for any delay. Where
there is a likely high risk to people, assess the duty to tell them without
undue delay. Record the assessment even if notification is not required.

Before adopting this procedure, name the incident owner and backup, confirm
the contact route and check that the organisation can meet these duties.
