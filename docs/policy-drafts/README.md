# Policy drafts for review

Prepared 29 October 2026 for roadmap item 51, after the 1.0.6 release.
These are review drafts, not adopted policies. They do not replace the
[current policy page](../POLICIES.md) or change the software licence.

| Draft | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Terms and fair use](TERMS.md) | MIT rights, responsible use, support and warranty |
| [Privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) | Microphone use, local files, online services and personal information |
| [Data protection procedure](DATA-PROTECTION.md) | Internal handling of support records, requests and incidents |
| [Accessibility statement](ACCESSIBILITY.md) | Available features, limitations and feedback |
| [Voluntary support](SUPPORT.md) | Joanne's tip jar, payments, privacy and mistakes |
| [Review and evidence](REVIEW.md) | Outstanding decisions, implementation checks and publication steps |

The proposed public-facing documents are TERMS, PRIVACY, ACCESSIBILITY and
SUPPORT. DATA-PROTECTION and REVIEW are internal working documents; they
must not be presented as adopted procedures or published as user policies.
The proposed public text assumes SEE Change Happen Ltd operates support
and receives tips. Confirm the actual payment recipient and controller
before adopting it; the label "Joanne's tip jar" does not settle either role.

Read the drafts as a set. They distinguish information the app processes on
the user's computer from information Joanne or the company actually
receives. They do not claim an accessibility audit, GDPR certification,
tax exemption, a refund guarantee or a paid support service.

No changes to application behaviour, release files or website deployment
are part of this drafting work. The release tag must remain untouched.
