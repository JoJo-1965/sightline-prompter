# UAT Preview 09

Unpublished Windows x64 preview, app 1.1.0, documentation revision 10.
Includes UAT 08 and the combined PC feedback batch. This is a UAT before RC.

1. Open Settings. Getting started is first, above Prepare to record. Its
   First setup guide identifies your script and configuration, explains the
   next steps and links to settings. Scroll its centre pane; navigation,
   the sample and footer remain fixed.
2. Open Debug > Status and recovery. Check listening status and recovery
   controls. Restart uses the applied speech/microphone settings; enable
   command listening in Voice > Commands first if required.
3. Show the head guide. Guide adjustments and Guide off share a compact
   control group beside the recording controls. Guide off hides the guide
   and adjustments without changing its saved position.
4. Open Recording > Slate and sync. Off, Flash and beep, and Flash only must
   align without narrow word stacks. Try each selection and Apply.
5. Enable Reading > Reading aids > Focus band. Spoken lines outside the band
   dim; production comments and inline directions keep their own appearance.
   Top and bottom margin fading still applies to all text.
6. Drag the main prompter to another screen. It must leave full-screen or
   maximised mode and become a centred normal window at 75% of the destination
   work area's width and height. Eye-line mode and a pinned monitor choice
   clear. Moving within the same screen retains the chosen size. Explicit
   monitor-menu placement still works as before.
7. Continue the [UAT 08 preparation and recording checks](PREVIEW-08.md),
   including promo slot 4, real framing/audio, OBS and physical Stream Deck.

Run **Launch Sightline UAT 09.cmd** from the preview folder to retain your
existing preview profile, pairings and scripts. The separate-profile launcher
is for another PC or an isolated trial. UAT 08 remains available for rollback.

Collect feedback as one batch. Software fixtures do not replace physical
camera/audio, cross-display dragging, recording or controller acceptance.
