# Joanne's tip jar

**Effective: 29 October 2026.**

If one of my free tools has helped you, you can leave a voluntary tip.
It supports my time creating and maintaining open-source work, including
Sightline Prompter and its Stream Deck plugin. You are welcome to use them
without tipping.

I drink black decaf and love a blueberry muffin. If you fancy stretching
to a Caesar salad, even better. Those are friendly ways to describe a
thank-you, rather than items you are buying or a promise about how each
payment will be spent.

## What a tip means

A tip is freely given. You receive no goods or services, licence upgrade,
priority support, advertising, membership or promised feature in return.
It is not an investment or a purchase of future development. Unless we
separately agree otherwise before payment, it supports Joanne's work
generally rather than being restricted to a particular product.

This is a personal support request for Joanne's work, not an appeal on
behalf of a registered charity. We do not offer Gift Aid or promise a
tax deduction. Calling a payment a tip or donation does not by itself
determine its tax treatment.

The payment recipient is SEE Change Happen Ltd, based in England, United
Kingdom. "Joanne's tip jar" is the name of this support request.

## Ways to give

Use [Stripe](https://buy.stripe.com/4gMeVd5GLgmZdx9btV5ZC01) or
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/joannelockwood). Check the amount, currency,
recipient and whether the payment is one-off or recurring before confirming.
Available options and payment methods are shown at checkout. Provider
fees may reduce the amount received.

You do not enter payment-card details into Sightline. Stripe or the other
payment service offered at checkout handles them. Its terms and privacy
notice apply alongside our [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md).

## Privacy and acknowledgements

The payment provider may share your name, email, message, amount and
transaction reference with the recipient for administration. Giving a
tip does not subscribe you to marketing. A receipt or a personal thank-you
may be sent in connection with your support.

Check Ko-fi's public-supporter and message visibility choices before
posting. We will not create a separate public supporter acknowledgement
using your details without asking first. Do not put confidential information
in a payment message.

## If something goes wrong

For an accidental or duplicate tip, email **info@seechangehappen.co.uk**
with the date, amount and transaction reference. Do not send a full card
number, security code or account password. We will review the payment and
explain the available options; we make no blanket promise that
every voluntary payment can be refunded.

For a payment you did not authorise, contact your payment provider promptly
as well. Any refund we arrange will use the original payment route where
possible. Nothing here limits your statutory or payment-provider rights.
