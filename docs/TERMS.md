## Your scripts and recordings

You keep your rights in your scripts, recordings and exported work.
Using Sightline does not give Joanne or the company ownership of them.
You are responsible for having permission to use the material you load,
and for deciding whether and how to record other people.

Sightline helps with prompting and timing. Use your usual recording tool
for production audio and video. Optional scratch audio is a microphone
recording for synchronisation; review it before sharing an edit pack.
Speech recognition, word timings, take suggestions and level measurements
can be wrong. Check the results before relying on them for a finished edit.

## Fair use of shared project spaces

Be considerate in issues, discussions and support messages. Describe the
problem clearly, respect other people and keep personal information,
passwords, API keys and other people's recordings out of public posts.
Do not use these channels for harassment, spam, impersonation or attempts
to gain access to someone else's systems.

Project maintainers may moderate abusive or unsafe contributions or limit
access to project support channels. These community expectations do not
limit the rights granted by the MIT licence. There is no software usage
quota or requirement to tip.
