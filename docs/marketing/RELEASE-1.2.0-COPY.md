## Short introduction

Sightline keeps your script near the lens while you can still see the call,
slides or camera picture behind it. Choose voice following, steady scrolling
or talking points. Prepare a consistent shot, control the take and hand useful
context to your editor. Free and open source, with optional AI, MCP, OBS and
Stream Deck connections.

## What's new, grouped for a release announcement

- **Read comfortably:** complete styling for speech, headings, cues and comments;
  custom themes, editable transparency and a consistent Settings workspace.
- **Prepare the shot:** shared manual/local head-position feedback, audio advice,
  a reviewed test and a saved reference for returning after a break.
- **Stay in control:** a recoverable bottom guide toolbar, full assignment lists,
  one Connections home, clearer OBS state and deliberate recording permissions.
- **Work with your AI:** local MCP and API access, a structured script brief,
  refreshed demos and a useful edit pack. AI is optional; timing needs review.
