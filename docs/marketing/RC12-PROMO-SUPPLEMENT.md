# RC12 promo and demo update

This supplements the approved promo script. Do not overwrite earlier promo
performances, client scripts, take logs or snapshots. The existing video shows
an earlier candidate; it has not been recut into an RC12 demonstration.

Suggested narration: “Prepare your frame, check the room and your voice, and
review a test before you record. The voice check waits until you speak. Need a
quick correction? Pause, edit the paragraph and save, then carry on. You can
also say the active dialog's enabled button labels, or choose Sightline as
your wake word.”

Capture these replacement UI inserts from RC12:

- Small prompter toolbar with the slot picker and one upper-right utility group;
  a wide view shows nine slots and the same button alignment.
- Full editor transitions Close → Cancel → Close after Undo or Save.
- Room baseline, waiting-for-speech message, a normal voice sample and its
  advisory result. The sample ends after speech or at ten seconds.
- Enabled Check again spoken in the foreground dialog, with gold feedback.
- Start from section heading and a continuous Voice passage across a soft stop.

Use the new short sample for a rehearsal. A real production clip still needs
an explicit recorder start, a sync cue captured after Record, and an explicit
finish. Do not claim improved recognition is guaranteed for every accent or
microphone, or that a level gate is speech-model training.
