# RC10 promo and demo capture update

Apply this guidance to the next recording of the approved promo. Preserve the
existing recording/script snapshots and pointing/HUD plan.

- Show Voice reaching a section-ending stop: the next spoken opening appears
  at the reading line and follows without another Go. The heading stays above.
- Capture the gold HUD direction just above the reading line. The pointing
  targets retain their established head-relative positions.
- Before speaking that next opening, demonstrate Again returning to the section
  just completed. After delivering the new passage, Again retakes that section.
- Demonstrate an explicit spoken Pause and Go as deliberate controls. Explain
  that Scroll still honours scripted stops and countdowns.
- Finish the recorder explicitly after the final performance. Confirm the pill
  and saved file; keep sync cues after recording starts.
- Keep the RC9 paragraph-spacing example and describe catch-up speed as display
  movement. Recognition remains dependent on the microphone and speech engine.
