# Help, website and messaging audit

31 October 2026 · Sightline 1.1.0 / private Stream Deck 0.2.0 · documentation
revision 1. Feature scope is frozen. This audit corrects documentation and
marketing; it does not approve publication or physical production acceptance.

## Outcome

The product has a coherent audience and purpose: camera-led speakers and solo
creators, and course authors/L&D teams making modular learning. Operators and
editors are a supporting audience. The lead is **The teleprompter that works
with your AI**: structured authoring, optional agent control and editing
handover. **Your words. Your pace.** is the delivery benefit. AI remains optional,
not an installation requirement or an embedded video editor. Joanne confirmed
that AI-led positioning should remain a USP during this audit. See the
[audience and evidence reference](POSITIONING.md).

Joanne's final clarification is the product definition: a traditional
teleprompter with voice control, AI/OBS/Stream Deck/pad/phone controls, and
optional scripting directives. A favourite AI can configure and control it
through an authorised local agent. The overview now starts there, rather than
making the editing handover sound like the entire product.

These are product-fit hypotheses, not validated customer demand. The useful
differentiation is the combination of see-through prompting, pacing/control
choices, script directions and editing evidence. No competitive study supports
an exclusive “only”, “best” or “unique” claim.
