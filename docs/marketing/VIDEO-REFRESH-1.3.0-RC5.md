## Pickup instructions

1. Confirm the actual recorder is running, then make the recorded clap/slate.
   Wait for Listening before the spoken run-in. Keep the same camera crop,
   saved head reference, reading view, lighting and microphone route.
2. For each pickup: warm up naturally, read the existing H2 title, settle for a
   beat and deliver the complete body sentence(s). Leave space at both ends.
   Keep titles as headings; do not duplicate them into body copy or speech notes.
3. Capture one current pointing/HUD performance and real app cutaways. Keep
   presenter-relative directions, existing graphic labels and the actual
   visible gesture aligned with the matched direction plan.
4. Review earlier recordings by captured section title and script snapshot.
   Numbering changed between drafts. Listen to the previously flagged talking-
   points opening and handover wording before selecting them; recognition
   output alone does not establish a misread or a usable take.
5. Export each new script snapshot, take log, edit pack, available scratch audio
   and original masters together. Verify recorded-audio timing and picture sync.
   The editor removes warm-up/title material using structure and actual footage.

Keep the approved 1920×1080 frame, consistent content width, translucent text
