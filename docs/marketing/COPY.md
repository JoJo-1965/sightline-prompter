## Short introduction — public-release safe

Sightline Prompter is a traditional teleprompter that works with your AI.
Voice controlled, see-through and free: read at your pace, with phone and pad
controls within reach. Your favourite AI can prepare scripts and, through an
authorised local agent, configure and control it. Optional script directions
steer the prompting. By a speaker, for speakers. Use it without AI too.

<https://joannelockwood.co.uk/sightline>

## Speaker/community introduction — public-release safe

I've built Sightline Prompter with Claude Code because I wanted my script near
the camera, my slides still visible and room to speak at my own pace.

It's for speakers, content creators and people recording e-learning: a
see-through prompter with voice following or steady scrolling, voice commands,
a phone remote and take information for the edit. Use your own script or an
optional AI assistant. Keep your usual recording tool.

Free under the MIT licence, and always will be. Try it, tell me what gets in
your way, and help shape what comes next.

<https://joannelockwood.co.uk/sightline>
<https://github.com/JoJo-1965/sightline-prompter>

## Optional social carousel storyboard

This is reusable copy for a future designed carousel, not an existing exported
social asset. Keep the eight-slide website carousel distinct from this story.

1. **The teleprompter that works with your AI.** A familiar prompter your
   favourite AI can configure and control through an authorised local agent.
2. **Keep your view.** Your script near the lens; slides or a call behind it.
3. **Choose your pace.** Voice following or steady scrolling, with stops for
   the moments you want to hold.
4. **Keep control.** Voice, phone, keys or a floating pad. Optional Stream Deck
   is downloadable from the app release, with Marketplace submission pending.
5. **Leave clues for the edit.** Mark keepers and hand over take references and
   estimated alignments alongside the recording.
6. **Use AI if it helps.** Prepare scripts or interpret the edit pack with your
   chosen assistant. Basic prompting works without it.
7. **Try Sightline.** Free and MIT-licensed. Use the verified public download
   at joannelockwood.co.uk/sightline; use current 1.1.0 screenshots.
