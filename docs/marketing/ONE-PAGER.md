# Sightline Prompter

[Printable one-page PDF](sightline-one-pager.pdf)

## The teleprompter that works with your AI.

A traditional teleprompter, built for your workflow. Free, open source and
see-through, with voice control and voice following or steady scrolling.
For speakers, content creators and learning teams recording to camera.
Your favourite AI can prepare scripts and, through an authorised local agent,
configure and control Sightline. Its optional scripting directives steer
the prompting; take information helps your external editor afterwards.

**By a speaker, for speakers. By a content creator, for content creators.**

**Your words. Your pace.** Keep your script near the lens while seeing the slides or call behind it.
Choose a steady pace or let the script follow your voice. Pause, retake and
mark a keeper without losing the thread of what you wanted to say.

### From preparation to handover

- **Prepare:** open Markdown, Word, PDF or a Google Docs link. Add optional
  sections, stops, beat marks and named markers. AI can help write the script;
  it is not required.
- **Present:** use voice commands, a phone remote, keyboard or floating pad.
  Adjust text, contrast and mirroring to fit your screen or beam-splitter rig.
- **Review:** keep section/take references, markers, keeper choices and
  estimated word alignment in an edit pack for your external editing workflow.

### Preparation and control in 1.2.0

Shared manual/local head-position feedback, audio checks, a reviewed short test
and a saved reference support consistent shots. A recoverable bottom guide
toolbar keeps controls within reach. Optional OBS control and Stream Deck 0.2.0
fit an existing recording workflow; local MCP lets an authorised AI help.

**Sightline 1.2.0 + Stream Deck 0.2.0 — 4 November 2026.** Download
both from the app release. The plugin source stays private; Marketplace
listing is pending. The app targets Windows, macOS and Linux; Stream Deck is
for Windows and macOS. Check your own recording and sync route.

### Know what you are getting

Free under the MIT licence, and always will be. No Sightline account or
subscription. Local speech works offline after the model download; optional
Deepgram sends audio online. Your chosen AI service has its own terms.

Keep your usual recorder for production video and audio. Sightline can save
optional microphone scratch audio for sync; timing and captions need review.
Head detection supplements manual placement; check capture exclusion and sync
on your actual setup. Windows is unsigned and Mac is ad-hoc signed/unnotarised;
the download page links illustrated installation help.
