## The essentials

- Local speech recognition processes audio on your computer. Voice
  following can save recognised words as text and script-word timings.
- **Always** wake-word listening can run while stopped or off air. Turning
  voice commands off does not turn off other microphone features.
- Optional scratch audio records the microphone for synchronisation,
  including off-script passages. It can be included in an edit pack.
- Deepgram sends audio to an online service when you choose it. Its data
  handling differs from local recognition; read the privacy notice first.
- The phone remote, local API and Stream Deck connection are optional.
  Protect their keys and review what an AI assistant can access.
- Sightline does not record video. Keep using your production recording
  tool, and check timings and measurements before relying on them.
