Settings

Scrolling
Slate

A sync point for the edit. Before each take the screen flashes white and shows the section and take number for a second; with the beep, your microphone records it too. The take log notes when each slate went off, so every take can be lined up with the recording. Start reading after the beep.

Audio references and levels

While the prompter runs, Sightline records the microphone to a plain audio file beside the take log, even off script. An editor can line your real recording up with the take log by matching the two soundtracks, with no slate needed. It stays on this computer, and goes into the edit pack when you export one.

Measures your microphone through each take: its loudness, its loudest peak and the room's background noise. A take that clipped, was very quiet or had a noisy room gets a warning as it ends, in the take summary and in the take log and edit pack. It's the mic as Sightline hears it, before OBS or Riverside process it, so treat it as a guide.

Voice scroll sync

Voice commands

Say the wake word (below) then a command, and stop: pause, go, slower, faster, back, next section, from the top, again, marker, resync, bigger, smaller, or what can I say. With Always, the microphone listens for the wake word whenever Sightline is open, so "Prompter, go" starts it hands-free, in voice or steady scrolling (on this computer only; nothing is sent anywhere).

Recognition and microphone

Choose Voice on the top bar or in Prompting > Scrolling to follow your speech.

Sound-check display

The sound-check meter (Sound check in the Voice panel, or Ctrl+Shift+D) shows your mic's level, loudness and the room's background noise at the edge of the prompter. It hides while a take runs unless this is ticked.

Your words

Script words

Names and words it still mishears: type your word, then what it hears instead, with commas between. Each counts as your word wherever the script has it, and as itself everywhere else.

Command words

Choose the wake word or a command it misses, then type what it hears, such as “prompt her”.

Teach… listens while you say the word or command a few times, with a pause between, and adds what it hears. Changes apply the next time it starts listening.

Claude Code, Codex or another AI can then load scripts, run the prompter, and list, name and export your recordings (File > Recordings).

Stream Deck

Its own key, for buttons only: it can play and pause, move through the script, switch slots and show what's happening, but it can't load scripts, change settings or touch your recordings.

Phone remote

Scan this with the phone's camera, then add the page to the home screen for next time.

Only a device with the code in the QR code can control the prompter. The first time, Windows may ask whether to allow Sightline Prompter on the network: allow it on private networks. New code disconnects any phone already connected. Turn this off when you don't need it.

OBS Studio

Off by default. Disabled means no OBS connection or commands. Disabling or quitting Sightline leaves any OBS recording running. Apply saves these choices.

In OBS 28 or later: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings. Enable its server and authentication. No additional OBS plugin is needed. Use 127.0.0.1 for OBS on this computer. Remote connections use unencrypted WebSocket: use a trusted network. The password is kept separately using your operating system's protected storage.

OBS disabled

Apply changes before testing. Recording permissions and the recorded slate are in Recording > OBS recording.

OBS recording and sync

Connect OBS in Recording > OBS connection first. These permissions apply when OBS integration is enabled.

Leave this off for status only. Normal Play, Pause, Again and On air do not start or stop OBS. Record take waits for OBS to confirm recording before starting the prompter.

Needs recording controls and a supported OBS version and recording format, such as Hybrid MP4. Chapter failures leave prompting and recording running.

Optional, on this computer only. With OBS stopped, Apply then Prepare adds a dedicated source above this scene. It records a flash and 1 kHz tone on audio track 1; include that track in your recording. Prepare again after restarting Sightline or changing scenes. The head guide stays separate.
