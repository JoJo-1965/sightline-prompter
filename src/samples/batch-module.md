# Sample: a modular script for batch recording

[A sample for practising section-by-section recording. Each section has its
own target, and ends with a pause, so you can stop, reset and go again.]

## Section 1 · Cold open {target: 0:20}

[Say: "Section one". Pause.]

Have you ever walked out of a meeting and thought of the perfect thing to say,
about ten minutes too late?

Most of us have. And most of the time, it isn't because we didn't care. It's
because nobody gave us a moment to think. {pause}

## Section 2 · Why pace matters {wpm: 135}

[Say: "Section two". Slower and warmer.]

{marker} When we speak too quickly, people stop listening to the words and
start listening to the rush.

Slowing down a little gives your audience room to keep up. It also gives you
room to breathe. {stop}

## Section 3 · Three habits {target: 0:20}

[Say: "Section three".]

Here are three small habits that help.

First, pause before your key point, not after it. Second, finish your sentence
before you look at your notes. Third, when you lose your place, stop, smile,
and find it. Nobody minds.

{pause: 3s} Those three habits will carry you a long way. {stop}

## Section 4 · Close {target: 0:10}

[Say: "Section four". Look straight at the camera.]

Thank you for practising with this sample. Try the next section again, and see
whether you land on your target time. {pause}
