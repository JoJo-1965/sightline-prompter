## Opening

Good morning, and thank you for joining me.

Today I want to talk about something we all do, every single day, without
really thinking about it. We decide who gets heard.

## The everyday moments

It happens in meetings, when one voice fills the room. It happens on calls,
when the quiet person never quite finds a gap. And it happens in the small
moments, the corridor chat, the message that never gets a reply.

{pause: 3s}

None of this is usually deliberate. Most exclusion is thoughtlessness, not
malice. But the effect on the person left out is just the same.

## What we can do

So what can we do? We can notice. We can make the gap, and hold it open. We can
ask the person who hasn't spoken yet what they think, and then really listen.

[slower]

Small choices, made every day, are how culture changes.

{pause: 2s}

## Close

Thank you. I'd love to hear how you get on.

{stop}
